A simulated terminal screen. The chessboard, pieces, move list, material bar, ripples, captures, and trails are rendered by changing Braille dot-matrix glyphs inside a fixed terminal buffer. Enter an ASCII seed and press Start to replay the same match. Leave the seed empty to generate a random 100-character seed. Replay mode makes the same seed reproduce the same match; Live mode allows engines to use their native randomness and time-based search. Press 1, 2, 3, and 4 for 0.5x, 1x, 2x, and 4x playback. Press R to reroll with a new random seed. Press J and K to scroll the move list. Click Play Again after the match ends to replay the current seed.
